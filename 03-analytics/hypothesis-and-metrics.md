## Pre-work · Hypothesis check
- **Role , who you are solving for (from M2):** Persona: Mara

Role

Mara represents financially constrained consumers who want to resolve their debt independently through Riverty's self-service portal. She has limited time, an uncertain or variable income, and cannot pay her outstanding balance in full. Her priority is not simply to start an instalment plan, but to understand whether one is affordable and sustainable before making a commitment.

She values clarity, transparency, and reassurance throughout the journey. Rather than avoiding repayment, she wants confidence that she is making the right financial decision. If the process feels unclear, asks for information without explanation, or leaves uncertainty about the consequences of missing a payment, she loses trust, abandons the journey, or contacts an agent for reassurance.

Mara is therefore not a reluctant payer. She is a motivated consumer who wants to do the right thing but needs enough confidence to commit to a repayment plan she can realistically keep.
- **Goal , what this user is ultimately trying to achieve:** Understand her repayment options and choose a sustainable instalment plan without needing agent support.
- **Friction / moment of misery , the specific pain blocking their goal:** Mara reaches the point where she must commit to a monthly repayment amount without knowing whether she can realistically afford it or fully understanding the consequences if she misses a payment.
- **Current workaround , the external tool or manual process they rely on (M2):** Call a agent and clearify questions
- **Problem Hook , your one-sentence framing of the business crisis (M1):** Today's journey loses money and agent capacity at two points: before an agreement is reached, and after it. Left unchanged, costs rise with volume, portfolio value erodes, and the headline metric (SSR) can mask the problem.
- **Value Proposition , the outcome your initiative promised to deliver (M1):** Create a transparent, guided repayment journey that helps consumers choose sustainable instalment plans the first time, reducing unnecessary agent interactions while improving long-term recovery performance beyond SSR.

- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Role: A consumer with limited time and an uncertain income who wants to resolve their debt independently through Riverty's self-service portal.

Goal: Resolve their debt through a repayment plan they can confidently afford and successfully keep.

Friction: Mara reaches the point where she must commit to a monthly repayment amount without knowing whether she can realistically afford it or fully understanding the consequences if she misses a payment. - **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Consumers cannot confidently commit to an instalment plan because they lack clarity about affordability and the consequences of their decision.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** Consumers confidently complete sustainable repayment plans through self-service instead of abandoning the journey or contacting an agent. - **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** Percentage of consumers who complete a self-service instalment plan without contacting an agent within 7 days.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Percentage of repayment plans that miss a payment within 60 days. This rate must not increase above the current baseline of 19%.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** Review the pilot after 8–12 weeks or once at least 1,000 consumers have completed the digital instalment journey.
