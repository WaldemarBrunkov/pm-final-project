# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A consumer with limited time and an uncertain income who is trying to deal with a debt
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Get a clear answer on whether a manageable payment plan is possible, and start one they can keep.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** They are asked to commit to a monthly amount they cannot predict, and they fear that one wrong answer or one missed payment will make everything due at once. So they under-report, abandon, or call for reassurance instead of finishing online.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Contact via telefon
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Open the self-service portal to check whether an instalment plan is available.

Try to estimate an affordable monthly payment without enough guidance, often second-guessing or under-reporting income.

Stop the journey when uncertain about the consequences or when the journey cannot accommodate their situation (e.g. unsuitable start date or missing documents).

Contact a collection agent by phone to ask for reassurance or clarification before committing.

Complete the repayment plan with the agent instead of through self-service.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The moment they have to commit to a monthly repayment amount without knowing whether they can realistically afford it or what will happen if they get it wrong.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** "They ask me what I can afford per month. My pay changes every month. Do I put my best month? My worst month? Whatever I put, I feel like I'm signing up to it forever." (P3)

This is reinforced by observed behavior: consumers under-report their income, abandon the journey, or call an agent for reassurance instead of completing the process online.
