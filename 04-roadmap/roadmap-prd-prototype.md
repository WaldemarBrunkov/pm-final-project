# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** A consumer with limited time and an uncertain income who wants to resolve their debt independently through Riverty's self-service portal.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** Percentage of consumers who complete a self-service instalment plan without contacting an agent within 7 days.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** Consumers cannot confidently commit to an instalment plan because they lack clarity about affordability and the consequences of their decision.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Percentage of repayment plans that miss a payment within 60 days. This rate must not increase above the current baseline of 19%.

The M3 funnel shows that 32.8% of all journey drop-offs occur during income proof upload, making it the single largest abandonment point. Based on this evidence, features that simplify or replace the document upload process should receive higher priority, as they are likely to have a direct impact on both completion rates and agent contacts.
